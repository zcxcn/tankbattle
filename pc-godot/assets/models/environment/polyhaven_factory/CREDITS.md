The original glTF, binary geometry, and 1K PBR images are redistributed under
CC0. This asset is commercially reusable and redistributable. The source
artist is credited voluntarily. No paid or editorial-only assets are used.

Game integration: original brick panels, modeled window openings and frames,
steel doors, roller shutters and cornices assemble the actual building faces.
Source meshes and materials are shared through MultiMesh batches per building;
Godot generates mesh LODs at import. Facade batches stop rendering after 175 m;
solid building silhouettes, collision and roofs remain visible. This is real
geometry with depth and UV-mapped PBR materials, rather than an image of a building.

Game-authored additions: warehouses with loading canopies, chimney factories,
setback offices, apartment balconies, curved-roof hangars, transformer stations,
repair garages, oil tanks, cranes and utility fittings. These additions are code
under the project's license and are not attributed to the Poly Haven artist.
